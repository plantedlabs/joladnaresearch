# Woodley Drumgoole: research gaps and prioritised next actions

Ranked by expected value per hour. Everything here is a next step; nothing in this file has been done.

## A. Trace the 1811 Woodley forward (highest value)

1. **Read the rest of Brunswick Chancery 1811-003 and 1850-003.**
   * Library of Virginia: https://old.lva.virginia.gov/chancery/case_detail.asp?CFN=025-1811-003 and
     `CFN=025-1850-003` (images are free).
   * For 1850-003, read the bill (images 0002–0006) and the decree. Look for any mention of Woodley:
     gift to a child, earlier sale, hire, death, or "carried to the South".
   * Adin Lynch's **will** is referenced ("agreeably to the Will of Adin Lynch dec'd"). Find it in the
     **Brunswick County Will Books**, c. 1839–40 (Library of Virginia microfilm; FamilySearch images).
2. **Adin Lynch's household and heirs, 1811–1840.**
   * Did any Lynch heir, buyer, or Linch/Lynch relative move to Louisiana or Mississippi?
   * Heirs named c. 1840: Robert H., James C., Benj. H., George G. and Grief H. Lynch; Mary Laffoon;
     Dudley House & wife; Buckner children.
   * Check the 1840/1850 Catahoula, Concordia and Ouachita parish censuses (LAGenWeb transcriptions are
     available) for these surnames, and the Brunswick County deed books for sales of "Woodley".
3. **The other eight indexed "Woodley" suits.** Read the index and then the images, prioritising:
   * 1846-028 (William Kirby trust)
   * 1849-001 (Henry House will 1849; House family in the Dromgoole circle)
   * 1847-025 (Benjamin Phipps)
   * Mecklenburg 1846-032 / 1852-161 (Charles Colley will 1845) and 1848-008 (James Batte)

   Record each Woodley's age or valuation, and his fate, to rule candidates in or out.

## B. Louisiana before 1870

4. **Catahoula Parish conveyance and succession books after 1839** (Clerk of Court, Harrisonburg; also
   FamilySearch microfilm and Full-Text Search). Search "Woodley", "Woodly", "Wood" as an enslaved given
   name, and "carpenter". The abstracts searched here stop at 1839.
5. **1850 and 1860 Catahoula slave schedules** (NARA M432/M653; LAGenWeb has 1860). Look for holders with a
   male aged about 41–46 (1850) or 51–56 (1860), plus a female and a child consistent with Polly and General.
   This only screens for candidates; it cannot identify anyone, so use it to choose succession records to
   read.
6. **Freedmen's Bureau, Louisiana** (FamilySearch collection 2333781; NARA M1905; Smithsonian transcription
   project). Check labor contracts, the Harrisonburg/Trinity/Vidalia/Monroe offices 1865–68, and marriage
   registers for Woodley and Polly.
7. **1867 Louisiana voter registration** (Catahoula), if extant. The 1878 list already gives VA 1804; an
   1867 entry might add a county or the date of arrival in the parish (some registers recorded residence
   duration).
8. **Original 1878 register** behind the LAGenWeb transcription. Confirm the entry and look for any
   residence or ward columns.

## C. Virginia families (hypothesis testing, not assumption)

9. **Finding aids blocked to automation; read manually:**
   * Duke Rubenstein, *George Coke Dromgoole Papers, 1767–1974* (plantation records; Brunswick court papers).
   * William & Mary Swem, *Dromgoole Family Papers*, Mss. 39.1 D83.
   * UNC Southern Historical Collection, Edward Dromgoole Papers.

   Search them for Woodley, Linch/Lynch and Jones. The repo README's claim of a "Canaan plantation 1854 slave
   inventory at Duke" was **not verified** in this pass.
10. **Library of Virginia, Virginia Untold: The African American Narrative.** Search "Woodley" for Brunswick
    and Mecklenburg free registers, deeds, and personal property tax lists.
11. **Brunswick County personal property tax lists, 1811–1840.** Check whether Adin Linch/Lynch's count of
    enslaved people drops in a particular year, which would date a sale or gift.

## D. Household and identity checks

12. **Original 1870 and 1880 census images:**
    * Confirm "Ryilla" vs "Rizella" and the 1880 parents' birthplaces (transcribed as Louisiana).
    * Check neighbours: formerly enslaved people often lived near former enslavers.
13. **General Drumgoole's 1933 death certificate:** informant, and the parents' birthplaces as given.
14. **Y-DNA context** (see `ydna_pipeline/`): the E-FTF75935 Block Tree and kit IN147690 bear on African
